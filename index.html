About

Production ownership with an engineering mindset

I am a Production Reliability Engineer at Continental Finance Company, supporting business-critical financial applications through priority incident management, technical triage, root-cause analysis, post-release validation, observability, and operational automation.

My strongest work is centered on making production operations more proactive. I connect application, infrastructure, database, website, release, and incident signals so teams can understand what changed, what is affected, and what requires action.

I communicate technical findings in business terms, partner with development and platform teams on durable improvements, and document repeatable procedures through runbooks, SOPs, and reliability practices.

How I work

  • Restore safely. Stabilize service before pursuing deeper optimization.
  • Use evidence. Correlate logs, metrics, SQL, releases, and user impact.
  • Fix the system. Convert recurring incidents into code, monitoring, or process improvements.
  • Automate toil. Replace repetitive checks with observable, supportable workflows.
